⚠ Read the "100% active" figure carefully

The program summary shows Upskill Bahamas at 100% active in the last 30 days. That is technically true but misleading: the platform stamps a learner's "last seen" timestamp at the moment they sign up, and this whole cohort enrolled inside the 30-day window. It measures signing up, not coming back.

The truer retention signal: 17 of 29 learners (59%) never returned after their first session, and only 1 has been active in the past week. The numbers on this page use behavior — return visits, videos, submissions — not the signup timestamp.

What the data says

  • 1
    Enrollment worked; onboarding didn't convert. 29 learners signed up in a tight burst (15 on launch day, Jun 19), but 59% never returned for a second session. The gap between "signed up" and "started learning" is where this cohort is leaking.
  • 2
    There's a real, committed core. 6 learners watched multiple lessons and submitted work; the top learner logged 76 page views. The product engages people who come back — the challenge is getting them back, not holding them once there.
  • 3
    The curve is decaying, not plateauing. Activity peaked Jul 7 and has fallen to near-zero this week. Without a re-engagement touch (email, a live session, a nudge), this cohort trends toward dormant.
  • 4
    We're flying blind on who they are. No intake survey ran for this program — location, education, and goals are all empty, and there's no pre/post measure. We can see what learners did but not who they are or whether confidence moved. An intake + exit survey would make the next cohort measurable.
Method & caveats. All figures pulled directly from the BCC Academy production database on July 15, 2026, for the Forte Bahamas program (AI Literacy track). Behavioral metrics (returns, page views, videos, submissions) come from activity telemetry, which began recording June 29, 2026 — activity before that date is not captured, so early-cohort numbers are a floor, not a ceiling. "Returned" counts learners whose last-seen timestamp is more than 10 minutes after account creation. "Active this week" is the trailing 7 days. This program has no intake or exit survey, so no demographic or confidence data is available. n=29 enrolled learners; percentages are of that base.
