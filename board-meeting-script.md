## MAKE CRISTINA LOOK LIKE A GENIUS (1 min)

"The entire ed-tech industry right now —
billion-dollar platforms, VC-backed startups —
is trying to solve one problem:

how do you prove your students actually grew?
Not clicks. Not completions. Real skill growth.
Tied to real outcomes.

The answer is data. Specific data.
Where they started — captured in an intake survey.
What they produced — every submission, every reflection, every quiz.
Where they ended — a post-survey, a skills profile, a certificate.
And what happened after — did they get the job?

You can only collect that data if you own the platform.
Most organizations don't. They're renting it from someone else.

Cristina said build our own in October.
That decision is why we can answer that question
and our competitors can't.

Every reflection, every submission, every survey response —
that's ours.
That's the proof."
