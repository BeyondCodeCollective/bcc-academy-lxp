Events, registration & instructor access

Answers to the team's questions, checked against the platform as it actually runs today. Screenshots are the live admin panel; learner names and email addresses are blurred.

Navigation below means Admin panel → Manage

Events & workshops

How do you add events & workshops to the LXP?

There is no separate "event" object — an event or workshop is a course. Manage → Manage courses → New course. Build it manually, paste a link to import it, or describe it and let the AI draft it. Each session holds a title, content, resources, a meeting link and a recording, and edits go live with no deploy.

How do you create registration pages?

Manage → Landing pages → New. The page goes live at bccacademy.io/bcc/<your-slug> with an eyebrow line, headline, subhead, accent color, cohort dates, a session schedule and a signup form wired to a course. Eventbrite checkout can be embedded right on the page.

For apply-first or selective programs, use Manage → Applications → New instead — that gives you a form at /apply/<slug>.

What do you, as an admin, need to share to get sign-ups?

One link, whichever fits:

  • bccacademy.io/bcc/<slug> — the landing page, for open registration
  • /apply/<slug> — the application form, when you are selecting participants
  • A personal invite link from the Invites page — a magic link, no password

How do you create event communications?

Manage → Announcements. Post to one course or the whole program; it lands on the learner dashboard, emails enrolled learners who haven't opted out, and fires a push notification. It expires on a timer you set. Confirmation, invite, acceptance, application-received and certificate emails all send on their own.

Can you schedule reminders? Not built

Announcements send the moment you post them. Two things partly cover the gap: registration emails carry an "add to calendar" link, so the learner's own calendar does the reminding, and the Join button surfaces on the day of the session. A real scheduled reminder — "email everyone 24 hours before" — is new work.

Registration data

How do you track registration numbers?

Manage → Signups. One row per person per course, pulled from every source at once — landing page form, Eventbrite, staff invite, allowlist — with a running count and a stage of Waiting → Signed in → Enrolled.

Applications shows total and new-since-you-looked counts per form, and Attendance shows who actually turned up.

How do you pull registration data?

CSV export exists on Attendance, Insights/Surveys and Participant locations. Signups and Applications are on-screen tables only — no export button on them yet.

If someone cancels their registration, how is that handled?

There is no learner-facing cancel button. An admin removes them from the course in People, which also revokes their invite and allowlist row so they don't quietly re-enroll at next login. An Eventbrite refund does not remove them automatically — that stays manual. /privacy/withdraw is a data-deletion request, not a registration cancellation.

In person, virtual, and the website

In-person registration versus virtual

Same course object either way; what changes is the meeting link.

  • Virtual — put the Zoom link on the session. Learners join inside the LXP, attendance imports from Zoom nightly, recordings import hourly.
  • In person — leave the meeting link empty, put the address in the session content and on the landing page, take attendance by hand.

Participant ZIPs collected at signup roll up into Manage → Participant locations, which is what we use for geographic reporting.

How do we connect events to the website for recruitment?

The landing page is the public web page — link it from the marketing site and from social. Its form captures a "how did you hear about us" field, which is how we see what is working. Eventbrite listings can embed checkout on the page and flow into the same funnel.

The three real gaps

  1. Scheduled reminders — no way to queue an email ahead of a session.
  2. Program-admin access to registration data — Signups and Applications are locked to super-admins.
  3. Instructor-only facilitator guides — no private field for them.
