13 / 23NEXT · MARKETING vs PRODUCTION
// 14 MARKETING vs PRODUCTION TWO FUNCTIONS, NOT ONE×
//right now we're one production team — we need both

Marketing decides. Production delivers.

Marketing · sets direction

The story & the strategy.

  • Brand storytelling & fundraising — the bigger initiatives (Keeny).
  • Decides what we say, why, and which campaigns move the mission.
  • Owns the direction the whole org rallies behind.
Production · executes

The content & the output.

  • Captures & creates everything we shoot (Marc).
  • Templatizes & ships on-brand (Ashley) — a constant stream into the pipeline (Joel).
  • Surfaces the moments — a camper's story — back up to Marketing.

Think Netflix: one global brand team sets the story, each show has its own manager funneling up. Or Soho: a big idea from the center, local teams execute bottom-up. Marketing sets the story; Production makes it real, at volume.
