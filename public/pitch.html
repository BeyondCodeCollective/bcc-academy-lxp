07 · the survey system

Matched pre · mid · post.
Same questions, every stage.

The platform asks the same confidence statements at the start, midpoint, and end of every cohort — so gains can be measured precisely, not estimated.

Two pipelines

Authenticated — for logged-in students. Required gates between login and dashboard.

Public — for walk-ins, workshops, and alumni. No account needed.

Nine question types

Text · ZIP · radio · multi-select · Likert · dual-Likert (before/now) · month-year · date · consent.
