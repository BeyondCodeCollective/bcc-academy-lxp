01
Student-Facing

Student Experience

Log in and the platform already knows what you need to do next. No generic home screen — every student sees something different based on where they actually are.

📊
Personalized Dashboard
The dashboard reads the student's current state and puts the right next step front and center. Finishing onboarding, a pending survey, an active week, a new track to enroll in — whatever it is, that's what they see. No two students land on the same screen.
Track Grid View
All enrolled tracks in a grid. Students see their current week, progress per track, and can jump straight in.
Progress Tracking
Weeks completed vs. total weeks, live. Both the student and their admins see the same numbers.
Self-Paced Track Mode
Some tracks don't run on a cohort calendar. Weeks unlock as students work through them, not on a fixed schedule.
Announcement System
Admins can drop an announcement and it surfaces in each student's What's New feed — on their dashboard and the relevant course page.
Submission Workspace
Each week has prompts students respond to and attach files or links. Every submission is saved and reviewable from the admin panel.
Weekly Reflections
Students write a short reflection at the end of each week. Admins can read all of them.
AI Tutor Assistant
Each program gets its own AI tutor with a custom prompt. It knows the curriculum, speaks in the program's voice, and every conversation counts toward the student's engagement score.
Workshops & Recordings Library
Past sessions live in the platform too. Recordings, Lunch & Learns — not buried in someone's Google Drive.
Completion Certificates
Students who finish a track get a shareable certificate with a unique URL. It's theirs to keep and link to — verifiable, branded, permanent.
MASS Wraparound Track
Weekly coaching and wraparound support lives in the platform. Students get a dedicated track that doesn't interfere with their technical curriculum.
Pathway Profile (Assessment)
Students complete a 3-module psychometric assessment that identifies their archetype, work style, and motivations. Results are stored in their profile and visible to facilitators. See Section 08 for full detail.
Resources Page
A curated resource library per program — links, tools, and references students can access at any point in their track, separate from weekly content.
Accessibility Controls
Font size adjustment and read-aloud (text-to-speech) controls built into the platform. Available from the sidebar footer on every page and inline on the assessment — no browser extensions, no workarounds.
What's New Feed
One consolidated feed on the dashboard home — new announcements, instructor feedback on your work, and upcoming office hours in a single stream. Replaces scattered banners, shows nothing when there's nothing new, and stays off pages that already surface the same info so it never duplicates.
Calendar Sync
Subscribe to your whole schedule from Apple, Google, or Outlook with one link, or drop any office-hour session straight into Google Calendar with a single click.
Notification Preferences
Students choose which emails they get — new announcements and instructor feedback — from a settings page. On by default, off whenever they want.
02
Content Management

Curriculum & Content

The curriculum system handles everything from a 12-week cohort to a single workshop. You configure it; the platform handles the rest.

Weekly Track Structure
Each week has a title, description, objectives, session schedule, reflection prompts, submission prompts, and optional video links. Everything in one place.
Drip-Release Content Gating
Weeks lock until a date you set and open automatically. Students can see what's ahead, just not get into it yet.
Sequential Week Gating
Self-paced courses can require students to finish one week before the next unlocks — a guided path instead of a wall of open weeks. Flip it on per course right from the admin course editor (no code), or leave it off so every week stays open.
Track Types
Supports both weekly (semester-style, cohort-paced) and single-event (one-off workshop or intensive) track formats within the same platform.
Track Phases
Group weeks into phases — Foundation, Core, Workshop, Exit. Students see the arc, not just a flat list of weeks.
Prerequisite Enforcement
Track A can be locked until Track B is finished. The platform handles the check. Nobody has to do it manually.
Office Hours Integration
Instructors can add office hours or drop-in sessions to any track page with dates, times, and descriptions.
Session Video Recordings
Link a recording to any week. Students who missed the live session can catch up.
Flexible Submission Controls
Some weeks don't need a submission. You can turn it off per week so the experience stays clean for students.
03
Data Collection & Enrollment

Onboarding & Access

From first click to first class — application forms, intake surveys, and enrollment gates are built in. No chasing people down after the fact.

📋
Survey Gating System
Mark a survey as required and it blocks track access until the student completes it. It shows up on their dashboard. Demographic data, consent forms, pre-program assessments — all collected before they get to the content, without anyone having to follow up manually.
Intake Gates
Tracks can require an intake form before students get access. Each track can have its own, and you can stack them across programs.
Flexible Question Types
Radio buttons, multi-select checkboxes, open text. Each question can be required or optional.
Pre & Post Surveys
Configure a survey that runs before the program and another that runs after. Compare the answers to see what actually changed.
Public Survey Tracking
Surveys can run without an account. Responses from prospective students get tracked separately from enrolled ones.
Program-Specific Skipping
Students who transfer between programs don't get asked to redo surveys they already finished.
Welcome Video Onboarding
Each program can set a welcome video that plays when a student logs in for the first time. Presenter's name, custom message — makes it feel like someone's actually there.
Admissions Funnel
Applicants move through a review queue. Admins accept or pass, and accepted applicants get a magic link invite automatically — closing the loop from application to enrolled student.
Allowlist Access Control
Every program maintains an allowlist. Only approved emails can create an account. Combined with invite links, you control exactly who gets in and when.
Magic Link Authentication
No passwords. Students click a link, they're in. No app to download, no password to forget — just a magic link sent to their email that signs them in directly. Anyone already enrolled can always request a fresh link, even if their email was never on the original invite list.
Unified People Hub
One place to add people, one button: invite by email (allowlist + one-click link) or add an account directly for a known instructor or admin. No more hunting across separate Allowlist, Invites, and Add-person screens.
Onboarding Pipeline View
The roster shows everyone at every stage — Allowlisted → Invited → Joined → Active — in one list. You can see exactly who hasn't signed up yet, with the live count of people still pending.
Bulk Cohort Invites
Send one-click invites to a whole allowlist in one tap. Idempotent and resumable — re-running only sends what hasn't gone out, never re-pinging people who already joined. Remove anyone from the pending list inline.
Branded Domain Email
Sign-in links and invites send from the program's own verified domain (e.g. bccacademy.io) for trustworthy, deliverable email — with an automatic fallback so a login can never be blocked by an email hiccup.
04
Tracking & Metrics

Attendance & Engagement

Attendance is one signal. We track four. Admins get a score per student built from what they actually do in the platform.

Four-Pillar Engagement Scoring
Every student gets a score built from four things: showing up, submitting work, writing reflections, and using the AI tutor. Admins see one number per student, with the breakdown behind it.
Session-Level Attendance
Tracked per session, not per week. Programs that run two sessions in one week track both separately.
Admin Attendance Marking
Instructors mark attendance from the admin panel or via API. The record captures who marked it and when.
Real-Time Engagement Scores
Scores update as data comes in. No exports, no batch jobs — open the tab and see where everyone stands.
Alumni Tracking
When a student finishes, they move to alumni status. Their history stays intact and they stop showing up in the active cohort.
Last Seen Tracking
The platform records when each student was last active. Admins can spot who's gone quiet without waiting for an attendance cycle to catch it.
Self-Paced Progress View
Self-paced courses have no class to attend, so they get a Progress view instead of attendance: a per-student × per-week grid showing who watched each recording and uploaded their work, with running totals.
Switchable Student Views
Roster, Attendance (or Progress, for self-paced), and Submissions are one-tap peer views — never stacked. You see one clean list at a time instead of the same students repeated down the page.
05
Operations

Administration & Analytics

Program staff can run a cohort without filing a single ticket. Everything is in the admin panel.

🗂️
Tabbed Admin Interface
Students, Attendance, Submissions, Reflections, Engagement, Survey Insights, Tutor, Lunch & Learns — each tab only loads its data when you open it. The panel stays fast no matter how large the cohort gets.
Student Roster Management
Full student list with enrollment state, track assignments, and quick actions. Everything about a student in one view.
Submission Review
Browse and filter all submissions by track or student. See the prompt responses, attachments, and timestamps in one view.
Reflection Review
Read all student reflections, filtered by track or student. A good way to spot who's struggling before they tell you.
Survey Insights & CSV Export
Response counts are in the admin panel. Export everything to CSV — enrolled students and public responses alike.
Tutor Interaction Tracking
See how many messages each student has sent to the AI tutor. Useful for spotting who's using it and who hasn't touched it.
Lunch & Learn Management
Add and manage recorded sessions directly from the admin panel. Title, presenter, description, date, link — done.
Cross-Program Admin
Super-admins see all programs in one view. No switching accounts, no separate dashboards.
Engagement Scoring Dashboard
Per-student engagement scores with the full breakdown. See exactly where someone is strong and where they've gone quiet.
Survey & Form Links Panel
The admin home surfaces every survey and application form link for the program in one place — pre-survey, post-survey, intake, application forms. Copy and share in one click.
Assessment Results View
Admins can pull up any student's Pathway Profile results — archetype, work style, motivations — directly from the admin panel. Useful for cohort coaching and track placement decisions.
Feature Toggles
Admins can enable or disable features per program or track — the Pathway Assessment, AI Tutor, specific tabs — without touching code. Lets you roll features out gradually.
Program Builder
Super-admins can create and edit programs directly from the admin UI — name, branding, tracks, cohort settings. No code deploy required to launch a new program.
Per-Track Insights Dashboard
Drill into a specific track and see completion rates, engagement trends, survey response breakdowns, and tutor usage — all scoped to that cohort.
Cohort Groups View
Students are organized by track cohort in the admin panel. Each group row links directly to that cohort's student view — no searching, no filtering.
Single Course Hub
The admin home is the one place to run every course — each row offers Manage and Open-student-view, and a consistent Manage menu reaches course, landing-page, resource, and tool settings from anywhere.
Hide / Show Courses
Hide a course from the admin and catalog without deleting anything — fully reversible. Keeps placeholders and finished courses out of the way while preserving all their data.
06
Platform Architecture

Multi-Program Architecture

This isn't one program running on a platform. It is the platform. Multiple programs, each with its own identity, domain, and configuration — all managed from one place.

Branding & Identity
Per-Program Branding
Each program has its own logo, colors, tagline, SEO settings, and Google Analytics ID. None of it requires touching the code.
Custom Domain Support
Programs run on their own domain. Students who visit an old URL get redirected without losing their session.
Enrollment & Access
Cohort Configuration
Each program sets its own cohort name, start date, and total weeks. Programs run on completely different schedules with no interference.
Invite Link Enrollment
Programs can require a track-specific invite link at signup to control exactly who gets into what.
Compliance & Safety
COPPA Compliance Controls
Programs serving minors can enable COPPA requirements at the program level. It's a switch, not a rebuild.
Program Data Isolation
Student data stays in its program. Admins only see what belongs to them, even when programs share infrastructure.
07
Trust & Safety

Security & Access Control

Student data is locked down by program. Access is role-based at every level. The platform doesn't cut corners here.

Role-Based Access Control
Four roles: Student, Instructor, Admin, Super-Admin. Each sees only what they're supposed to see. Students never touch admin features.
Program-Scoped Data Isolation
Attendance records, enrollment data, submissions — all scoped to the student's program. An admin from one program cannot see another program's data.
Supabase Auth Foundation
Login and session management run on Supabase Auth. No custom credential storage, no rolling our own auth.
Admin Action Audit Trail
Attendance records log who marked them and when. Changes to student data have a paper trail.
Input Validation & Sanitization
Every identifier gets validated on the server with strict format rules before anything touches the database.
Performance & Caching
Static pages use ISR. The admin panel only loads tab data when you open it. The platform stays fast as cohorts scale.
Privacy & Data Withdrawal
Students can submit a data withdrawal request directly from the platform. The request is logged, routed to an admin, and confirmed with a dedicated flow — GDPR-ready without needing an external tool.
PII Access Audit Log
Every time a super-admin accesses student PII, it's logged with a timestamp and identity. Full audit trail for compliance.
Preview as Student
Super-admins can preview the portal exactly as a student sees it — and it's a real restriction, not just hidden menus: while previewing, admin pages and admin actions are fully blocked. One tap in, one tap back out.
08
Learner Intelligence

Pathway Profile Assessment

A purpose-built psychometric tool that helps students understand who they are as learners — and helps facilitators understand who's in the room. Not a quiz. A real instrument.

🧠
Three-Module Assessment Engine
The assessment runs three sequential modules: how you show up (Likert-scale strength inventory), how you work (forced-choice scenario pairs), and what drives you (motivation and pathway orientation). Questions shuffle on every session to prevent pattern-matching. Scoring is pure computation — no network calls, no delays. Results are available the moment the final module completes.
Archetype Scoring
The engine scores each student against a set of learner archetypes and surfaces a primary and secondary type with a confidence level. Low-confidence and broad-high results get their own language — the system doesn't overclaim.
Work Style Profile
Module 2 forced-choice scenarios map to a work style profile — how someone prefers to collaborate, handle ambiguity, and pace themselves. Separate from archetype, useful on its own.
Pathway Orientation
Module 3 identifies what kind of path fits the student right now — building, exploring, stabilizing, leading. Informs track recommendations and coaching conversations.
Results Profile Page
Students get a full results page with their archetype narrative, work style breakdown, and pathway orientation. Collapsible sections let them go as deep as they want. It's theirs — saved permanently to their account.
Resume from Any Point
Progress is saved after each module. If a student closes the tab mid-assessment, they pick up exactly where they left off — no starting over.
Admin Results Access
Facilitators can view any student's full results profile from the admin panel. Track placement, coaching prep, cohort dynamics — all informed by real data, not gut feel.
Feature-Flagged Per Track
The assessment is enabled or disabled per track via the feature toggle system. Not every cohort needs it — turn it on only where it fits.
Public Preview Mode
Stakeholders and partners can experience the full assessment at /assessment-preview with no login required. Results are scored and displayed live — nothing is saved. Good for demos, onboarding calls, and partner presentations.
09
Prospective Learners

Public Tools & Discovery

The platform doesn't start at login. Prospective students can explore tracks, take a quiz, apply to a cohort, and discover their pathway — all before creating an account.

Pathway Explorer
Public-facing pathway pages let prospective students explore what each learning path covers, who it's for, and what they'll be able to do afterward. No account required.
Learning Style Quiz
A short public quiz helps prospective students find the right track. Email-capture at the end feeds into the admissions pipeline — results emailed automatically, no admin action needed.
Public Survey Links
Pre-program surveys can run before a student has an account. Public submissions are tracked separately and automatically merged into the student record when they enroll.
Cohort Application Forms
Program-specific application pages are public URLs. Anyone can apply — no account required. Submissions land in the admin panel for review.
Assessment Preview
The full Pathway Profile Assessment is available publicly at /assessment-preview. Stakeholders, partners, and prospective students can experience it end-to-end without logging in.
Program Join Links
Each track has a shareable join link. Click it, create an account, and you're enrolled — no admin intervention unless the program uses the admissions funnel.
Lead Capture & Instant Alerts
The public homepage "Learn More" form captures prospective students with no login. Each signup is stored and fires an instant email alert to the team, with reply-to set to the lead so you can respond directly.
Cinematic Landing Hero
The homepage leads with a full-bleed background video (autoplaying on desktop and mobile) behind the brand and call to action — a living first impression instead of a static splash.
