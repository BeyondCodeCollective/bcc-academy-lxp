# Phase 4: accessibility (axe-core, WCAG 2.x A and AA)

21 page states x 2 viewports (42 scans), production build, logged-out and staged roles. Raw data: test-results/audit/a11y.jsonl. Scans do not fail the suite.

| Rule | Impact | Nodes | Pages | Example selector |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| color-contrast | serious | 67 | apex landing, login, generic apply, apply security-plus, apply home-for-summer, apply sbft (closed), privacy, privacy withdraw, quiz, instructor admin home, admin people tab, course builder new, student course overview (desktop and mobile) | `.tracking-wider.text-white\/40.text-\[11px\]` |
| select-name | critical | 4 | admin people tab (both viewports) | `.relative:nth-child(2) > select` |
| aria-hidden-focus | serious | 6 | mobile only: instructor admin home, admin people tab, course builder, student course overview, student session page, empty learner home | `.transition` (off canvas mobile menu keeps focusable children) |

Clean (zero violations): campaign landing, public survey, help, terms, 404, events, student session page (desktop).

Fix order: (1) raise text-white/40 at 11px to at least 4.5:1 (white/60 or larger), the single pattern behind most contrast hits; (2) add `aria-label` to the people tab select; (3) make the mobile nav drawer `inert` when closed.
