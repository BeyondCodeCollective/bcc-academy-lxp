# Denise's rules (written down for the first time)

Denise has run enrollment at Riverbend for eleven years. Nobody wrote any of
this down until now. Every rule has a reason; some of the reasons are load-bearing.

## Programs and ages
- **After School (ASP):** grades 3–6, ages 8–12. Sites: Riverbend Main (RB1), Eastside (RB2).
- **Teen Center (TN):** ages 13–18, grades 7–12. A 12-year-old in 7th grade waits
  until their 13th birthday, then moves over. Sites: RB1, RB2.
- **Summer (SUM):** opens for enrollment on 1 April. Anything before that goes on
  the pending list with a friendly note. Do not tell them they're enrolled.

## Capacity
- RB1 After School: 60. RB2 After School: 45. Teen Center: 50 per site.
- When a site is full, the child goes on the waitlist in order of request date.
  Siblings of currently enrolled members jump the waitlist. ("It's the only way
  parents with two kids can make pickup work.")

## Pickup authorization
- Any member under 13 needs a signed pickup authorization form (PU_AUTH = Y)
  before their first day. No form, no first day. Not negotiable. This rule
  exists because of an incident in 2019 that Denise will not discuss.
- Teens (13+) do not need one.

## Fee waivers
- A fee waiver needs a free/reduced lunch letter or a staff referral. Note which
  one in NOTES. Never ask a family for income on the phone.

## Duplicates
- Same guardian phone with a different child is usually a **sibling**, not a
  duplicate. Same child re-entered is a duplicate: keep the earlier record.

## Things that always go to a human
- Anything mentioning custody, court orders, allergies, medication or an IEP.
- Anything from a school counselor about more than one child (they're usually
  flagging a situation, not just enrolling).
- Anyone asking about volunteering, jobs or donations (wrong inbox, be kind,
  forward it).
- Any email where the grade and the birth date don't agree.

## Confirmation
- A confirmation email goes out only after Denise or the coordinator has looked
  at the record. Drafting the email is fine. Sending it is a human's job.
