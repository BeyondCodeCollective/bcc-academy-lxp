# Discovery conversation with Denise (fictional, 12 minutes, lightly edited)

**You:** Can you just show me what happens when an enrollment email comes in?

**Denise:** Sure. So it lands in the enroll inbox. Or my inbox. Or Marcus's,
if the parent knows him from the front desk. Or the school counselor sends it
to the principal, who forwards it to me. I'd say forty different people email
us about enrollment in a normal month.

**You:** Okay. Then what?

**Denise:** I open it, I read it, I figure out which program they mean. Half
the time they say "the after school thing" and I have to work out from the age
whether that's ASP or Teen. Then I open the export.

**You:** The spreadsheet?

**Denise:** The spreadsheet. It comes out of the old system, the one from 2009.
The columns are all abbreviations because the person who set it up had a
character limit or something. PRG_CD is program code. PU_AUTH is pickup
authorization. GRDN_PH is guardian phone. FEE_WVR, fee waiver. You learn it.

**You:** How long does one enrollment take you?

**Denise:** If it's clean? Ten minutes. Retype the name, DOB, grade, program,
site, phone. Check the age is right for the program. Check the site isn't
full. Check we've got the pickup form if they're under 13. Draft the
confirmation. But they're not usually clean. Missing birth date, no phone,
"either site is fine", form says attached and there's nothing attached. Then
it's twenty, thirty minutes with the back and forth.

**You:** How many a week?

**Denise:** Spring rush, sixty or seventy. Quiet months, fifteen.

**You:** What's the worst part?

**Denise:** The retyping isn't the worst part, honestly. The worst part is
when I'm out and someone else does it and gets it wrong. Because the rules
aren't written down anywhere. They're in my head. Like, same phone number
different kid, that's a sibling, not a duplicate. Everyone else flags it as a
duplicate. Or a counselor emailing about three kids at once, that's never
just an enrollment, that's a situation, you call them.

**You:** Why does the pickup form matter so much?

**Denise:** *(pause)* We had an incident in 2019. I'm not going to go into it.
The form is non-negotiable. No form, no first day.

**You:** Has anyone tried to fix this before?

**Denise:** They built a portal last year. Parents were supposed to enroll
themselves online. Forty-something families use it. Out of eleven thousand.
So now I've got the emails and the portal. It's more work, not less.

**You:** Why don't parents use it?

**Denise:** Because emailing works. They email, I reply, done. The portal
wants them to make an account, verify, upload, and half of them are doing
this on a phone in a car park at 5:45. And it doesn't know the rules either.
It let a 12-year-old enroll in Teen Center. I had to call the mum.

**You:** If I could take one thing off your plate, what would it be?

**Denise:** Read the email and fill in the row for me. Correctly. Flag the
weird ones. Draft the reply. I'll still click send. I want to click send.

**You:** Why do you want to click send?

**Denise:** Because when it goes wrong, it's my name on it. It should be my
name on it.
