# Forward Deploy: The FDE Program — Leadership Brief

**Proposal.** A five-week, hands-on program that teaches our people to do what
a Forward Deployed Engineer does: take one workflow we do the slow way, decide
where AI belongs in it, have an AI build the smallest thing that works, prove it
with a number, and put it in a colleague's hands. No one writes code. Claude
Code builds; our people supply the judgment.

**Why now.** Every organisation can buy the same AI tools. Roughly 95% of AI
pilots show no measurable business impact (MIT, 2025), and it is almost never
the model's fault. Pilots die because nobody decided where AI belonged, nobody
built for the unhappy paths, and nobody owned the result. This program trains
the three things that fix that. The skill is industry-agnostic; the domain
knowledge our people already have is the edge.

**What a learner walks away with**
- A working AI agent on a real workflow here, in use by a colleague for at least a week
- A one-page writeup: the broken workflow, what was built, what changed, the number
- A five-minute demo they have delivered, through the failure cases
- An eval report: 50 test cases, pass rate, every failure investigated, result in hours saved, errors caught or days recovered
- A handover so it outlives them

**What the organisation gets.** Deployments, not slides. A pilot cohort of
three to six people produces three to six measured automations on real work,
plus a leadership report with baseline and after numbers and quotes from the
colleagues using them.

**Shape.** 13 sessions of about an hour, self-paced in Claude Code, over five
weeks, with a 45-minute weekly group check-in and a demo day with the sponsor in
the room. Facilitator time: about two hours a week.

**Cost.** A paid Claude plan per learner for the duration, plus about 13 hours
of each learner's time and about 15 minutes from one colleague per learner in
week one. Check current plan pricing at claude.ai before quoting.

**Guardrails.** No real personal data about customers, clients, members,
employees or anyone else enters the AI during the program; practice data is
synthetic and each learner's own sample is de-identified and approved by
whoever owns the data. Anything built drafts, decides and logs; it never sends,
deletes or changes a system of record on its own. A human clicks send. Workflows
that need a new system, or touch payroll, protected records or anything
regulated the learner doesn't control, are excluded.

**Completion.** Defined in `RUBRIC.md`: all sessions done, deployment used by
someone else for a week, writeup shared, demo delivered, handover done, rubric
score 18/27 or better with no zero. Ownership is mandatory: if nobody else uses
it, it's not complete.

**Status and the ask.** Content is complete and published at
https://youngfonz.github.io/course-builder/forward-deploy-course/. It has not
yet been run by a learner. The ask: approve a **three-person pilot** on the
five-week schedule, and put demo day on your calendar. Pilot results, not this
brief, are what we'd bring back for a wider rollout.
