## Watch for, all the way through

- Anyone who has not spoken in 20 minutes. Bring them in by name.
- Anyone who says "I'm not technical." Note who and when. That sentence is what the whole course exists to disprove.

- Anyone who asks a question about their own job. That is the strongest signal in the room.
